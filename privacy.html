Crisis Hour

Privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Crisis Hour is made by Daniel Barnard. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: the game does not collect any.

What the game collects

Nothing. Crisis Hour has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect, send or share personal data.

Your progress

Your campaigns, Archive, achievements and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game removes them from the device.

If you back up your device with Apple, your progress may be included in that backup under your own Apple settings. If you use the game's "Save backup" to export a file, that file is yours, and it goes only where you choose to save or send it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy covers the information it holds.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it only to answer you, and we do not pass it on. We keep your email for as long as it is needed to answer you and to fix the problem you reported. You can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to the same address.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted by GitHub, which may record technical details such as visitors' IP addresses in its own logs.

Children

Crisis Hour is not aimed at children, and it collects no information from anyone of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
