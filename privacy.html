Crisis Hour

Privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026

Crisis Hour is made by Daniel Barnard, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for any personal information described in this policy. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: the game does not collect any.

What the game collects

Nothing. Crisis Hour has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect, send or share personal data.

Your progress

Your campaigns, Archive, achievements and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game removes them from the device.

If you back up your device with Apple, your progress may be included in that backup under your own Apple settings. If you use the game's "Save backup" to export a file, that file is yours, and it goes only where you choose to save or send it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy covers the information it holds.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you. You can turn this off in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. Emails are stored with our email provider. We use them only to answer you, which is in both our interests, and we do not pass them on. The "app details" the game lets you copy contain the game's version, the campaign code and how far you have played, and nothing personal. We keep your email for as long as it is needed to answer you and to fix the problem you reported. You can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to the same address.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted by GitHub, which may record technical details such as visitors' IP addresses in its own logs.

Children

Crisis Hour is rated 16+ on the App Store and is not aimed at children. It collects no information from anyone of any age.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, by writing to crisis.hour@danbarnard.com. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: crisis.hour@danbarnard.com.
