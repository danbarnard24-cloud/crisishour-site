Crisis Hour

Support

If something has gone wrong, or you have a question about the game, email dan@danbarnard.com.

What to include

  • What happened, and what you expected to happen.
  • Which campaign you were playing.
  • The app details from the game: open Settings, find Support, choose "Copy app details" and paste them into your email.

Before you delete the game

Please do not delete Crisis Hour to fix a problem. Your campaigns and your Archive are stored only on your device, and deleting the game deletes them too.

Keeping a copy of your progress

In Settings, "Save backup" lets you export your campaigns and Archive to a file, and import that file later or on another device. It is worth exporting a backup before you change phones.

Accessibility

Crisis Hour supports Larger Text, VoiceOver, Voice Control, Reduce Motion and increased contrast, which you turn on in your device's Settings. Every decision in the game can be made without sound or vibration.
